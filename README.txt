----------------------------------------------------------------------
GAMENAME: PROJECT: FLORA GUARD
----------------------------------------------------------------------
it is an action-packed sci-fi survival game. As a designated Guardian, you are tasked with the ultimate 
mission: protect a critically rare, bio-engineered plant from relentless automated robot determined to eradicate organic life.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

w - move forward
s - move backward
a - move left
d - move right

f - punch
v - switch camera view
space - jump, skip cut scene
x - screenshot
Esc - return to main menu
